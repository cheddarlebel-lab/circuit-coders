01The Opportunity

Semper Fi Sports Cave has built something most shops never do: a 4.6-star reputation across real Google reviews and a loyal local following in Fallbrook — minutes from Camp Pendleton. Customers already love the shop. There's just one gap holding it back online:

Your Google listing has no website attached. When someone searches "card shop near me" or finds you on Google Maps, there's nowhere for them to go — no way to browse your inventory, check today's hours, or see what just came in. Every one of those clicks is a customer you're paying nothing to reach, and currently can't capture.

The trading-card hobby runs on discovery. Buyers want to see the cards, trust the price, and know you're open before they drive over. A fast, professional website does all three — and it's the single highest-leverage thing the shop can add online.

02What We've Already Built For You

Rather than pitch you on what we could build, we built it. Your website is finished and live today — pull it up on your phone and look around. Here's what's in it:

A real brand, not a template

  • USMC scarlet & gold identity built around your actual Semper Fi shield logo — clean, premium, and unmistakably yours.
  • Mobile-first: looks sharp on the phones your customers actually use, fast-loading and modern.

An AI-powered card scanner & inventory system — the part nobody else has

  • Snap a photo of any card and the AI identifies it automatically — name, set, number, and category.
  • It pulls the card's live market price straight from TCGPlayer (raw cards) and eBay sold comps (graded slabs), so your prices are always fair-market and current.
  • Graded slabs get a PSA / CGC / BGS cert-verification link buyers can click to confirm authenticity.
  • A private admin dashboard lets you add, edit, feature, price, and remove cards in seconds — no tech skills needed.

A shopping experience built for collectors

  • A live "what's in the vault now" counter and category breakdown on the homepage that updates as you add stock.
  • Pro-grade filtered search: shoppers narrow by grade (PSA 10, PSA 9, BGS, CGC, raw), era, and price bracket.
  • A hover magnifier loupe so buyers can inspect high-end slabs in detail — exactly what they need before spending real money.
  • "Live market value" badges that reframe every price as a fair-market quote, not a markup. Instant trust.

The basics, done right

  • Your verified store hours with a live "Open Now / Closed" badge, plus a "follow our Instagram for holiday hours" prompt.
  • Click-to-call phone, one-tap directions, and direct links to your Instagram, Facebook, and X.
  • Built for Google: fast, search-friendly, and ready to be the website on your Google listing.
