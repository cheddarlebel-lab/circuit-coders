## ⚠️ HARD RULE — READ FIRST

Facebook page merge is **destructive and permanent**:

- The **losing page's reviews, posts, photos, and ratings are PERMANENTLY DELETED.** They do NOT transfer.
- **Only followers/likes transfer** to the surviving (kept) page.
- The merge **cannot be undone.**

Therefore the keeper MUST be the page that already holds the real review/post history. Never merge a review-rich page *into* a thin one. Get Jared's explicit written sign-off on exactly what is being lost before pressing merge.

### Facebook merge eligibility requirements (both pages must satisfy ALL):
1. **Same name** — pages must have effectively the same name. Rename the loser to match the keeper FIRST.
2. **Same category** — primary category must match.
3. **Same physical address / location** — the addresses must represent the same place.
4. You must be an **admin of BOTH pages.**
   - If admin access on a duplicate is unknown/lost, that page cannot be merged via self-serve — it must be claimed or reported as a duplicate to Meta first.
