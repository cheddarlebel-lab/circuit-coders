# Asking for Reviews — Staff One-Pager
*Toyota & Kia of Vero Beach · Circuit Coders*

The single highest-leverage thing you can do for the dealership's online reputation is **ask happy customers for a review at the right moment**. Here's the simple version.

## When to ask
- **Right after the high point** — keys handed over, a great service experience, a problem solved well. That's when people say yes.
- **Sales:** at delivery, once they're smiling in the new car.
- **Service:** at pickup, when the car's done and the bill matched the quote.

## How to ask (say it naturally)
> "If you've got 30 seconds, it would genuinely help my team if you scanned this and left a quick review. No pressure at all."

Then hand them the QR card. **Let them scan it in front of you** — that's when it actually happens.

## Which card to hand out — IT MATTERS
We route reviews to the platform that needs them most. Use the right card:

| Store | Hand out | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Toyota** | **Google** card | Google is where shoppers look first — keep it growing. |
| **Kia** | **CARFAX** card first, then **Yelp**, then **Google** | Kia has almost no reviews on CARFAX and Yelp — those are the weak spots dragging the brand down. Filling them moves the needle fastest. |

> Tip for Kia: rotate the three cards through the week so all three platforms fill up, not just one.

## The rules (important)
- **Never offer anything** — no discounts, no gift cards, no "spin the wheel" for a review. Google, Yelp, and CARFAX all ban incentivized reviews and will remove them (and can penalize the store).
- **Never ask only happy people in a way that filters** — just ask everyone who had a good visit. Don't screen.
- **Never write the review for them or tell them what to say.**
- If someone seems unhappy, **don't hand them a card** — get a manager. We fix it in person, not on a public page.

## What happens next
Circuit Coders monitors every platform and responds to every review within 24 hours — you don't have to. Your only job is the ask. Every review you generate is one we then amplify and protect.
