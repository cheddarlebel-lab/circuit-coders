## Frequently Asked Questions

**How far is Toyota of Vero Beach from Sebastian, FL?**
About 20 minutes — roughly 12 to 14 miles south on US-1, depending on which part of Sebastian you start from. It's a direct route with no highway switching required. From central Sebastian, take US-1 south through Wabasso and Gifford; we're on the east side at 1075 S US Hwy 1 in Vero Beach.

**How do I get there from Micco or Barefoot Bay?**
Head to US-1 and drive south. From Micco and Barefoot Bay you'll merge onto US-1 near the Sebastian River and continue straight south to our store. Most Barefoot Bay drivers reach us in about 20 to 25 minutes.

**How do I get there from Fellsmere?**
From Fellsmere, take CR-512 east toward Sebastian, then turn south on US-1. Expect roughly 25 to 30 minutes total, depending on the lights through Sebastian.

**Isn't Melbourne closer?**
For most of Sebastian, no — and even where the mileage is similar, the drive north on US-1 through Palm Bay and Melbourne routinely runs 40+ minutes with traffic. The trip south to Vero Beach is shorter, calmer, and more predictable.

**Do you offer a service shuttle, and how far does it go?**
Ask about our shuttle service and current coverage area, plus loaner availability for qualifying visits, when you schedule. Availability varies by day and job — call [772-569-8000](tel:7725698000) to confirm whether a ride home to Sebastian or Barefoot Bay can be arranged for your appointment.

**Do you deliver to Sebastian?**
In many cases, yes. Once your deal is finalized online, ask us about delivery to Sebastian, Micco, Barefoot Bay, or Fellsmere. Eligibility depends on the vehicle and your address, so call to confirm before you plan your day.

**Can I do the whole purchase online and only come down once?**
Yes. Build your deal, apply for financing (https://www.verobeachtoyota.com/finance.aspx), and value your trade from home. We'll have the paperwork ready so your one trip down US-1 is just to sign and drive.
