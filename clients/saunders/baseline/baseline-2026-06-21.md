## 3. Keyword Rank Snapshot — 15 tracked terms (rank_tracker.KEYWORDS)

**Method/caveat (read this before trusting the numbers):**
- **No CAPTCHA and no consent/cookie wall on any of the 15 searches.** All loaded
  clean.
- **Organic position** = 1-based position of the dealership's own result among the
  `<h3>` result headings (server-rendered, reliable).
- **`[near-me GEO WARNING]`:** the two "…near me" queries resolved to the
  **browser's physical location (Fallbrook / Carlsbad / San Diego CA)**, NOT Vero
  Beach. So "near me" rank is meaningless for this client from this machine — it
  shows California dealers. All explicit-city queries ("vero beach", "sebastian fl",
  "indian river county") resolved correctly to the Vero Beach SERP. **Recommend
  dropping the two "near me" keywords** or pinning a Vero Beach geo via the `uule`
  parameter in future runs.
- **Map-pack (local 3-pack) position is not reliably extractable** in automation:
  for the dealership's own branded/category terms Google shows the **knowledge
  panel** (right rail, single business) rather than an ordered 3-pack, and where a
  pack does appear it lazy-loads without stable ordered DOM nodes. So map-pack
  position is recorded as "KP" (owns knowledge panel) / "—" (not determinable)
  rather than a guessed 1-3. Organic is the trustworthy signal.
