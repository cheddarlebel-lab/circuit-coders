**Date captured:** 2026-06-23
**Client:** Saunders — Circuit Coders brand management
**Method:** Public Google Search + Google Maps reads only (claude-in-chrome). No logins, no contact, nothing modified. Every figure read off Google's own Search/Maps surfaces on this date.
**Purpose:** Sharpen the local-SEO offense and arm the monthly report. Tells us exactly what the drafted local-defense pages (Vero Beach / Sebastian / Indian River County) must beat.

## 1. Headline finding — the threat narrative has shifted

The original engagement premise was "Bev Smith Toyota runs indexed 'near me' landing pages targeting OUR city (Vero Beach)." **That is no longer true on the Toyota side, and it understates the threat on the Kia side.**

- **Bev Smith TOYOTA DROPPED its dedicated Vero Beach page.** The old `/toyota-dealer-serving-vero-beach-fl/` URL now **301-redirects to their Fort Pierce page** (`/toyota-dealer-serving-fort-pierce-fl.html`). A `site:bevsmithtoyota.com "serving vero beach"` search returns **zero** documents. Their only Vero Beach targeting now is incidental city-list stuffing on model pages ("serving Port St. Lucie **and Vero Beach**") and an EV "Beyond Zero" page. **The dedicated-page threat from Bev Smith Toyota is effectively gone.**
- **Bev Smith KIA is the real conquest threat.** They run a live, indexed `/kia-dealer-near-vero-beach-fl/` page (~520 words, real Vero Beach landmarks) that **surfaces on page 1 of "kia dealer vero beach."** This is the single strongest competitor asset found and the one our drafted Kia Vero Beach page must out-build.
- **Toyota of Melbourne** targets Vero only via **programmatic inventory pages** ("[Model] for Sale near Vero Beach, FL") and a homepage meta mention — auto-generated SRP-level pages, not optimized city landing pages.
- **Jenkins Kia of Cocoa** (north Brevard) runs a dedicated `/kia-dealership-near-sebastian-fl` page and ranks on "kia dealer sebastian fl" — a Sebastian-flank conquest threat we should note.

**Bottom line:** our client already wins #1 organic on every core branded-city term and out-rates both Bev Smith stores on Google. The exposed fronts are (a) **Kia volume vs Boniface Hiers**, (b) **Bev Smith Kia's Vero conquest page**, (c) **Sebastian Kia flank** (Jenkins Cocoa), and (d) **commodity terms** (oil change, used cars) where same-brand and aggregator pages beat us.

## 3. The Bev Smith template — exactly what to beat

Both Bev Smith conquest pages (the Toyota Fort Pierce "serving" page and the Bev Smith Kia Vero Beach page) use an **identical CMS template**:

- **Length:** ~450–520 words. (Our P0 spec is 1,400–1,800 — we win on depth by 3x.)
- **H1:** `[Brand] Dealer Near/Serving [City], FL | Bev Smith [Brand] in Fort Pierce`
- **5 H2 sections, always the same:** "Serving [City]" (one landmark paragraph) → Inventory → Financing → Service/Parts → "Shop [Brand] Vehicles."
- **Local hook:** one short paragraph of real landmarks. The Kia Vero page names **Indian River Citrus Museum, Walking Tree Brewery, Vero Beach Theatre Guild** — genuinely local, not stuffing. (We should match this quality and go deeper: Ocean Drive, Riverside Park, McKee Botanical Garden, Sebastian Inlet.)
- **What they LACK (our edge, confirmed):**
  - **No FAQ block** → we add `FAQPage` schema (rich-result eligibility they don't have).
  - **No visible JSON-LD / structured data** → we add `AutoDealer` + `FAQPage` (+ `AutoRepair` on service pages).
  - **No drive-time math** → they say "located to the north of Fort Pierce" but never quantify. We win with "15 min, not the 30+ min drive south to Fort Pierce / 1 hr to Melbourne."
  - **No review quotes** → we embed real attributed Google review snippets (we have the data pipeline).
  - **Generic boilerplate** ("our locally-owned dealership has served the Fort Pierce area for over 30 years") → recycled across cities. Our copy is genuinely city-specific.

## 5. Gap analysis — where competitors beat us & what our pages must do

### TOP 3 GAPS TO CLOSE

1. **Bev Smith Kia owns a live Vero Beach conquest page that ranks pg 1 for "kia dealer vero beach."**
   *Beat it with:* our drafted `/kia-dealer-vero-beach-fl/` at 1,400–1,800w (vs their ~520), `AutoDealer` + `FAQPage` JSON-LD (they have none), drive-time math ("the ONLY Kia in Indian River County — Bev Smith is 17 min south, Boniface is an hour north"), 4–6 FAQ, and 1–2 real attributed Google review quotes. Match their landmark quality (Citrus Museum / Walking Tree / Theatre Guild are local-legit) and go deeper. This is the #1 page to ship first.

2. **Commodity/service terms leak to aggregators AND a same-brand competitor.** On "oil change vero beach," **Vero Beach Mitsubishi's dedicated oil-change page outranks ours**, and Midas/Jiffy Lube/Walmart own the top. On "used car dealer vero beach," Mullinax Ford (4.8/3.4K) + independents own the pack; we're present but not in the local 3-pack.
   *Beat it with:* the drafted `/toyota-service-vero-beach-fl/` page (published menu pricing + `AutoRepair`/`FAQPage` schema — directly answers "service pricing clarity" review complaints and out-structures Mitsubishi) and the `/used-cars-vero-beach-indian-river-county/` CPO page. Lead service copy with the upfront-pricing angle no aggregator or Mitsubishi page offers.

3. **Kia volume + Sebastian/north flank vs Boniface Hiers (4.7/8,044) and Jenkins Kia of Cocoa.** Boniface beats us on rating + volume from the north; Jenkins Cocoa has a Sebastian page.
   *Beat it with:* (a) the drafted `/kia-dealer-sebastian-fl/` page leaning hard on drive-time ("20 min south on US-1 vs an hour-plus to Melbourne through Palm Bay traffic" — and Boniface is in West Melbourne, even farther) + `areaServed` Sebastian/Micco/Barefoot Bay/Grant-Valkaria; (b) **review velocity** — close the ~5,000-review Google gap to Boniface over time via the review-gen pipeline; it's the one metric where a far-north competitor genuinely outranks us, and it's the slowest to move, so start now.

### What we have that EVERY competitor lacks (lead with these in copy + the report)
- **Schema:** none of the audited competitor city pages show JSON-LD. Our `AutoDealer` + `FAQPage` (+ `AutoRepair`) is a structural moat for rich results.
- **FAQ blocks:** zero competitors run them on city pages.
- **Drive-time framing:** competitors gesture at geography ("located to the north of Fort Pierce") but never quantify; we put minutes on the page.
- **Review-snippet proof:** we can embed real attributed Google quotes; none of them do.
- **Highest Google rating in the local Toyota set and the south Kia set** — usable as a copy and report claim ("the highest-rated Toyota dealer on the Treasure Coast" is defensible at 4.5 vs 4.4/4.4/4.3).
