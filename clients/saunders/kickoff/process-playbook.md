## 1. Google Business Profile (two listings)

### Adding us as manager
1. An existing Owner of each GBP signs into Google → profile → **Business Profile settings → People and access → Add** → enter our email → role **Manager** → Invite. (Google sometimes restricts new invites to Manager-only; plan around Manager, not Owner.)
2. We accept the email invite — active immediately.

- **Roles:** Primary owner (one, transfers only) > Owner (adds/removes users, can delete profile) > Manager (everything else: edits, posts, reviews, photos, insights — cannot manage users).
- **7-day restriction:** new users can't delete the profile, remove other managers, or transfer primary ownership for 7 days. Day-to-day work (reviews, posts, info edits) works on day one.
- **Who:** OWNER must send the invite (5-min screen-share or click-path doc). AGENCY thereafter.
- **Turnaround:** instant; full powers day 7.
- **Failure modes:** dealer doesn't know which Google account owns the profile (ex-employee/old vendor is common). If access is lost: "Request access" flow → current owner has 3 days to respond → Google may release it. Budget 1–2 weeks for that path. Removing an old agency: any Owner can do it in People and access.
- Source: support.google.com/business/answer/3403100

### Editing services / hours / phones
- Single location: edit directly in Search/Maps → Edit profile. Most edits live in minutes; **phone/category/address changes may go "pending" review — up to ~10 days**, sometimes rejected and needing resubmit/support escalation.
- Bulk spreadsheet import (Business Profile Manager, business.google.com) requires **10+ locations** — we don't qualify at 2 rooftops. Just group both profiles under one organization in Business Profile Manager and edit each.
- **Who:** AGENCY (Manager suffices).

### Posts — cadence/limits
- Types: Update (rotates out of carousel after ~7 days), Offer and Event (live until end date).
- Limits: 1,500-char body but only first ~75–100 chars show pre-truncation; Offer/Event title 58 chars; image 1200×900 ideal (min 400×300).
- Cadence: 1–4/week; minimum 1/week so an unexpired Update is always visible. Dealer pattern: weekly inventory/service Update + monthly Offer (oil change/tires) with end dates.
- **Gotchas:** auto-rejection for phone numbers in text, stock/over-texted images; rejection reasons are vague — resubmit cleaner copy.
- **Who:** AGENCY, immediately.

### Review replies
- Any Owner/Manager via Search dashboard → Read reviews → Reply. No 7-day wait. Public, reviewer notified, editable anytime.
- Flagging fake reviews: Report review → days-to-weeks moderation, low removal rate; escalate via Reviews Management Tool.
- **Who:** AGENCY. Instant publish.

### Photo guidelines (dealer set)
- JPG/PNG, 10 KB–5 MB, ≥720×720 (floor 250×250); cover 1024×576 landscape (Google may crop/ignore cover choice); logo 720×720.
- Must be real, in-focus, unfiltered — heavy edits/stock get rejected. Shot list per rooftop: exterior with signage, showroom, service bays/lounge, team, current inventory.
- Customer photos removable only via policy flag. New photos take 24–48h to appear.
- Sources: support.google.com/business/answer/6103862; whitespark.ca GBP posts guide.

## 2. Facebook duplicate-page merge (Meta, 2026)

**Eligibility:** full-control admin access on BOTH pages; same/very similar names; same address if listed; often both pages must sit in the **same Meta Business Portfolio**; no pending merge requests; no page restrictions/strikes.

**Steps:**
1. Get full control of both pages. Auto-generated/unmanaged duplicates must first be **claimed** ("Is this your business?" flow).
2. **Rename-first:** edit the loser page's name to match the keeper (e.g., "Toyota of Vero Beach 2"). Big renames can be rejected — change gradually. Rename approval takes **~24h–3 days**. Align addresses too.
3. Go to **facebook.com/pages/merge** (canonical URL; menu placement in Business Suite shifts), pick keeper + loser, submit.
4. Eligible merges complete instantly-to-minutes; high-follower pages can queue **24–72h manual review**.

**Kept vs lost (IRREVERSIBLE):**
- Merged in: followers/likes, check-ins (deduped).
- **Deleted from loser forever: all posts, photos, videos, REVIEWS/recommendations, username, Messenger threads.**
- ⚠️ #1 dealer gotcha: if the "duplicate" has the better review history, **make IT the keeper**. Audit review counts on both pages before choosing.

**If merge option unavailable:** causes = task-access-not-full-control, name/address mismatch, pending merge, restrictions, or Locations-framework pages (must use Locations structure instead). Support: business.facebook.com → Help (chat unlocks with even tiny ad spend); expect days-to-weeks of canned replies. Fallback: pinned "we've moved" post on loser, then unpublish (followers lost).

- **Who:** AGENCY if granted full control via Business Manager on both pages; otherwise OWNER's page admin executes while we drive.
- **Turnaround:** 1–7 days end-to-end including renames; weeks if support escalation.
- Sources: facebook.com/business/help/249601088403018; reshiftmedia.com merge FAQ.

## 3. Yelp (Toyota has 3 fragmented listings)

### Claiming an unclaimed listing
1. biz.yelp.com → search business → "Claim this business" (free) → create Yelp for Business account.
2. **Verification = automated call/SMS to the listed business phone** (sometimes domain email). ⚠️ Dealership IVR phone trees break the robocall — **schedule reception to answer and relay the code**, or temporarily route the line.
3. Claim completes within ~24h; flagged cases 1–3 days.
- Stale claimed pages can revert to unclaimed and be reclaimed the same way. If an old vendor/competitor holds the claim: Yelp support ownership dispute — slow, needs proof of affiliation.
- **Who:** AGENCY drives; STAFF answers the verification call.

### Duplicates — report/merge (the Toyota ×3 problem)
- **No self-serve merge.** From the claimed Yelp for Business account → Support Center → "Report an issue" → submit **URLs of all 3 pages**, statement they're the same business, which is canonical, plus evidence (same phone/address). Also flag the dupes from the public pages.
- Yelp moderators **merge** (reviews typically consolidate into the survivor) or mark closed. They will NOT delete review history on request.
- Claim the duplicates too if possible — Yelp moves faster when the requester controls all pages. Check which duplicate ranks in Google before picking the survivor.
- **Timeline:** 3–5 business days simple; merges days-to-weeks; no SLA — follow up after a week.

### Review-reply rules
- Must claim first; reply via Yelp for Business dashboard only. Two channels: Public Comment (use for substance) and Direct Message.
- Responder profile needs a real name/photo of a business rep. **Never solicit or incentivize reviews — Yelp uniquely prohibits ALL review solicitation** (violations → Consumer Alert banner).
- Review disputes: flag from dashboard → days; only clear guideline violations removed.
- **Who:** AGENCY once added as a user in the dealer's Yelp Biz account.
- Sources: biz.yelp.com Support Center articles (claim, duplicates, DOs/DON'Ts).

## 10. BBB (Southeast Florida & Caribbean — West Palm Beach, (561) 842-1918)

### Responding to complaints
- Timeline: BBB forwards within 2 business days → **business has 14 calendar days** to respond → second notice if silent → ~30 days total closure window.
- Respond via the link in the BBB notification email or the free claimed-business portal on bbb.org. **Accreditation NOT required to respond.**
- Closing statuses: **Resolved** (consumer confirmed — best) > **Answered** (business responded, consumer rejected/silent — still counts as responsive) > Unresolved > **Unanswered** (worst) > Unpursuable.
- **Who:** AGENCY can draft/submit with the portal login or forwarded notification emails; OWNER must claim the profile / hand over credentials (no agency-of-record concept at BBB).

### Rating recalculation
- A+ to F on a 0–100 scale across **13 elements**; **complaint history is the heaviest** (volume vs size, responsiveness, resolution, timeliness, rolling **3-year window**).
- Recalc is **automatic**: flipping Unanswered → Answered restores points within days. **Answering ALL open complaints — even old ones — is the single fastest grade-repair lever**; an unanswered complaint alone can force an F, so clearing backlog can jump multiple letter grades. Complaints age off after 3 years.

### Correcting a profile alert (e.g., the licensing flag)
- Alerts are **editorial, placed by the local BBB** — contact BBB Serving Southeast Florida & the Caribbean (4411 Beacon Circle, West Palm Beach FL 33407) dispute-resolution/business-relations in writing with **cure documentation** (current license copy, or proof the license category doesn't apply). BBB verifies — often directly with the state agency — then edits/removes.
- **Who:** owner or authorized agent; have Jared CC'd or send a one-line authorization.
- **Turnaround:** days-to-weeks; state-agency verification is the long pole.
- **Gotchas:** BBB never removes truthful complaints on request (only 3-year aging or consumer retraction). Don't argue "defamation" — provide cure docs. Internal rule: never say "unlicensed" publicly; frame as a records gap.
