Local Search Management Agreement

Google Business Profile & Local SEO — David General Construction Co. (home of Wood Replacement Pros)

Prepared for David Varela, Owner — David General Construction Co.  ·  Prepared by Leo Lebel, Circuit Coders  ·  Date June 22, 2026

1Overview

This agreement covers ongoing management and optimization of the Google Business Profile (Google Maps / local search listing) for David General Construction Co., the licensed contracting business behind Wood Replacement Pros (CSLB #903775, Vista, CA, serving San Diego County). The goal is simple: make the listing complete, accurate, and active so the business shows up — and stands out — when local homeowners search Google and Google Maps for termite-report repairs, wood rot, fascia and eave work, and related services.

This agreement is independent of the Wood Replacement Pros website build and its existing subscription, and does not change either.

2One-Time Setup & Optimization

Performed once at the start of the engagement:

  • Profile claim & secure access — establish manager access to the Google Business Profile and remove any unauthorized or third-party managers.
  • Business description — professionally rewritten, leading with the CSLB license and the Section 1 termite-report / wood-rot specialty, including "home of Wood Replacement Pros."
  • Services — full services list built out (termite & Section 1 repairs, wood rot, fascia & eaves, sub-floor, drywall repair, interior structural repairs, remodeling, water & moisture control, and related).
  • Service areas — configured across San Diego County (Vista, Oceanside, Carlsbad, San Marcos, Escondido, Encinitas, Del Mar, Rancho Santa Fe, San Diego and surrounding communities).
  • Categories, hours & contact info — correct primary/secondary categories, business hours, and phone, with the home address kept hidden (service-area business).
  • Review short-link — a one-tap Google review link created and provided for use after completed jobs.
  • Photos — initial set of before/after and finished-job photos curated, oriented, and uploaded.
  • Listing consistency cleanup — name / address / phone (NAP) reviewed across major directories (Yelp, MapQuest, and others); corrected where we have access, with correction requested where a third-party platform or the owner must make the change. Some directories may require your action or the platform's approval.

3Ongoing Monthly Management

Performed each month for the duration of the engagement:

  • Listing upkeep — keep the profile accurate and fully optimized as services, areas, or details change.
  • Google Posts & updates — periodic posts (offers, completed projects, seasonal updates) to keep the listing active, which Google rewards.
  • Photo additions — new job photos added to the listing as you send them.
  • Review support — monitor incoming reviews, help craft responses, and provide tools/prompts to generate more reviews from happy customers.
  • Performance monitoring — track listing views, searches, calls, and direction requests.
  • NAP consistency — ongoing monitoring to keep the business name, address, and phone consistent across the web.
  • Monthly summary — a short plain-English recap of what was done and how the listing is performing.
