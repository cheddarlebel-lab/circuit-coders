Executive Summary

This audit was not commissioned. We ran it because a national sweep of low-rated franchise dealerships across 54 metros surfaced Humble Hyundai, and the shape of the problem turned out to be unusually simple.

Your BBB grade has exactly one stated cause. Not service quality, not the nature of the complaints — BBB lists a single reason for the F: "Failure to respond to 10 complaint(s) filed against business." That is the whole grade. We pulled the eight most recent complaints BBB publishes and every one of them is marked Unanswered, running from February 2024 through January 2026.

Meanwhile the store itself is doing fine. Google has you at 4.3 stars on 2,055 reviews after 24 years in business, with recent reviewers naming staff by name and describing patient, no-pressure sales. That is not a dealership with a quality problem.

The cheapest item in this document costs nothing and takes ten minutes: your Yelp page is unclaimed. It sits at 2.2 stars on 165 reviews with 59 more suppressed by Yelp's filter. Because nobody has claimed it, nobody at the store has ever been notified of a review there, and nobody can reply. That 2.2 has simply been accumulating, unattended, 2.1 stars below your Google rating.

One complaint in the file sits outside the reputation frame and we would rather flag it than sell around it: a customer describes submitting paperwork for a GAP policy refund after selling their vehicle and not receiving it. Unearned GAP refunds are regulated. Section 3 covers it plainly.

2The BBB F — One Cause, Eight of Eight Unanswered

Hard defect
BBB lists a single reason for your F: ten complaints were never answered.

Verbatim: "Failure to respond to 10 complaint(s) filed against business." There is no second reason. The profile carries an alert. 24 years in business.

We checked the eight most recent complaints individually. All eight are Unanswered, spanning 02/2024 through 01/2026 — nearly two years without a single published response.

This is the most fixable grade in our entire sweep, precisely because silence is the only input. BBB's algorithm punishes non-response far more heavily than the complaint itself — a business that answers all ten and resolves none would score materially better than where you sit now.

Ten unanswered at a store with 2,055 happy Google reviews is a routing problem.

Nobody running a 24-year Hyundai store decides to ignore ten complaints. Far more often the BBB notices land at an address nobody monitors — a departed employee, a general mailbox, a spam folder. That is a fifteen-minute diagnosis and it is the first thing we would check.

3What the Complaints Actually Say

We read the published texts rather than just the counts.

Stated plainly before anything else in this section.

Everything below is an allegation made by a member of the public on a platform that publishes complaints without adjudicating them. Circuit Coders takes no position on whether any of it is accurate, and we are not lawyers.

The one we would look at first — a GAP refund

A July 2025 complainant states they purchased a GAP policy, later sold the vehicle, submitted the required documentation, and spent over a week trying to obtain the refund. Unearned GAP refunds are regulated. We are not lawyers and this is not legal advice, but of everything in this document that is the item we would resolve first regardless of whether you ever hire us — a pattern of unreturned cancellation refunds attracts attention well beyond BBB.

A January 2026 complainant describes a related theme: being told a service contract could not be cancelled despite, in their account, the contract saying otherwise.

Theme two — new vehicles with early defects

  • A November 2025 buyer of a 2026 Santa Fe Hybrid Limited describes the electronics and screen failing in the first week, under 100 miles.
  • A July 2025 buyer describes a hybrid battery fault two weeks after purchase on a brand-new vehicle.

Neither is your manufacturing. Both are warranty situations where the customer's frustration landed on a public page and got no reply — which converts a Hyundai problem into a Humble Hyundai problem.

Theme three — vehicle condition and time in service

  • A February 2024 complainant alleges their dashboard was damaged while the vehicle was in the dealership's care — two long cracks — at pickup.
  • An October 2024 complainant describes waiting 3.5 hours for a navigation update that could not be completed, a promised callback that did not come, and a 90-minute drive each way.

The damage allegation is documentary and answerable from your own records. The 3.5-hour one is not a service failure so much as a communication failure — and it is exactly the kind that never reaches a GM, because the customer simply stops coming.

4What Is Genuinely Well Run

Credit where it is earned.

A store with real problems on the floor does not hold 4.3 stars across 2,055 reviews for 24 years.

  • 2,055 Google reviews at 4.3 is a solid base, and it is the raw material any recovery runs on.
  • Recent reviewers name staff individually — one describes being "treated with kindness and respect" with "many, many questions answered thoroughly and patiently"; another names a salesperson who "worked really hard for us and never gave up."
  • Your Google tags include "no pressure sales" (32 mentions). That is customers independently describing your floor, and it is the opposite of what an F implies.
  • The Google listing is actively maintained — an owner post went up four days before we looked.
  • 24 years in business. Longevity is the one thing no competitor can buy.

6The Program

Everything below is work we do, not advice we hand you.

Recovery sprint — the first 30 days

  • Claim the Yelp listing. Day one, free, ten minutes. Until it is done nobody at the store can respond to anything there.
  • Diagnose where BBB notices are landing. Ten unanswered complaints at a store with 2,055 happy Google reviews is almost certainly a mailbox nobody owns.
  • Answer the backlog. All 10 complaints drafted, reviewed by you, filed. Where a complaint is resolvable we pursue the resolution, not just the reply.
  • Escalate the GAP refund complaint out of the queue. It goes to you directly in week one with the internal question written out, before any public response drafts.
  • Pull records on the dashboard-damage complaint. It is documentary and answerable today — either disproved by the intake photos or it is real. Both outcomes are worth knowing.
  • NAP reconciliation across Google, Yelp, BBB, KBB, Cars.com, DealerRater, CARFAX, Apple and Bing.

Ongoing, monthly

  • Every review on every platform answered within 48 hours, in your voice, signed by a real person.
  • A review pipeline pointed at the unmanaged platforms — the fix for Yelp is not arguing with the reviews you have, it is giving the next several hundred satisfied buyers somewhere to go.
  • BBB complaint monitoring with a named owner, so the grade never regenerates from silence.
  • Listing integrity monitoring — new duplicates, drifting hours, changed numbers.
  • Monthly written report: what moved, what did not, what we recommend next. Plain English.

9Method

So you can check every number rather than trust it.

  • Discovery. BBB's public search API, filtered to F-rated new- and used-car dealers across 54 U.S. metros. 3,368 low-rated dealerships returned; 1,128 franchise rooftops after removing national chains and manufacturer corporate records.
