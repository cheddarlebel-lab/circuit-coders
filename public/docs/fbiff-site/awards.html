Categories

What we jury.

Feature

Narrative work over 40 minutes.

Short

Narrative work under 40 minutes — the heart of our program.

Documentary Feature

Non-fiction over 40 minutes.

Documentary Short

Non-fiction under 40 minutes.

Student

Work made while enrolled — judged against its peers, not against funded features.

Animation

Any length, any technique, from hand-drawn to 3D.

Category definitions above describe how the six juried awards were presented in 2025. Runtime rules for the 2026 edition are confirmed on FilmFreeway at the time of entry.
