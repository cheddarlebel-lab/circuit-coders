Our Story

Showing up for
film since 2007.

Founded in 2007 as the Fallbrook Film Factory, and soon after as the Fallbrook Film Festival, we re-branded in 2024 as the Fallbrook / Bonsall International Film Festival. What has not changed is the passion for independent film and the desire to give emerging filmmakers a real screen, a real audience, and a real jury.

We screen the most innovative and thought-provoking films we can find, from feature-length drama to experimental shorts, and we program for a room that includes first-time filmmakers and lifelong moviegoers sitting in the same rows.
