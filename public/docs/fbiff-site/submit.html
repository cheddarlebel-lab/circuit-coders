Submit

Send us your film.

FBIFF exists to give independent and emerging filmmakers a screened, juried platform. We accept all genres and all mediums — animation, horror, sci-fi, documentary and everything between.

Submit on FilmFreeway

Open your FilmFreeway entry

All submissions, deadlines and entry fees are handled on our FilmFreeway listing. Current deadlines and fee tiers are always shown there.

Pick your category

Feature, Short, Documentary Feature, Documentary Short, Student or Animation. If you are unsure which fits, submit to the closest one and tell us in the notes.

We watch every entry

This is a one-screen festival with a real jury. Selections are programmed into a single Saturday running 10 AM to 8 PM.

Selected filmmakers are invited

Come to Fallbrook, sit in the room, and meet the audience watching your film. It is a small theater, and that is the point.

Questions

Talk to a person.

Submissions questions, print traffic, accessibility needs, or a note about your film — write to us directly.
