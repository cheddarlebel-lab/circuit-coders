Plan your day

Drop in, or stay
for all of it.

One pass covers the whole day, so you can come for a single block and come back after dinner. Shorts run through the morning and afternoon; the features anchor the middle and the end of the evening.

Downtown Fallbrook is walkable from the theater, with parking on and around Main Avenue.
