Program

The 2025 program.

Twenty-three films screened at the Historic Mission Theater on a single Saturday, in running order. The 2026 program is being juried now — this is what a day at FBIFF looks like.

Day pass · $20
10:00 AM

Solace

Childhood best friends Tony and Raul see their bond change forever when Raul shows Tony a memory box of old photos — and reveals he owns a weapon. A story about breaking through the darkness and finding solace in someone else.

19:09
10:30 AM

Al Maram

Maram, a mother and journalist, is displaced from her city, just as her ancestors were seventy-five years before. She defies the danger of war and long absences from her children to keep reporting the truth, even at the cost of her life.

5:14
10:45 AM

The Sweet Perfume

A student named Ruqiya becomes dizzy and nauseous during a national exam because of a perfume allergy, but the invigilator will not let her leave. Another student notices her weakness and begins to exploit it.

14:53
11:00 AM

The Most Beautiful Place on Earth

A camping-trip reunion on a long-forgotten beach turns from a short vacation into a survival game. Who can you really trust — and do memories fog your perception?

32:41
11:45 AM

Glitched

Nora, a young teen of color devastated by the death of her mother, makes a decision with lasting consequences that carries her into a realm science cannot prove.

13:45

Returning to Lulu

Eva has spent ten years in and out of institutions. Back now, she has to conquer both her fears and her insanity in order to return to her daughter, Lulu.

1:05:00

The Demon Car of Proctor Valley Road

On their first date, high schoolers Brett and Erica ignore her father's warnings about local legends and take the infamous Proctor Valley Road shortcut — and end up in a high-speed race for their lives.

12:15
1:30 PM

I Can Smell a Rat

Ten-year-old Maribel is told she must play Hamlet in the school play two days before the show. She has never acted before, and spends the scariest night of her life with all her fears coming alive.

16:54
2:00 PM

Ride Share

A ride-share driver picks up a fare that turns out to be his ex and her new partner. She takes the opportunity to address some unresolved issues.

10:31
2:15 PM

Thesis

A father must decide between a scientific breakthrough and saving his family.

12:58

Fluff and Furious

A fluffy pup, inspired by its wild relative, sets out to prove it is more than just a soft ball of fur.

Adas Falasteen

"Palestine Lentils" follows a Palestinian chef using his craft as a shield against the famine caused by the war on Gaza, preserving resilience and hope within his community.

7:55
3:00 PM

Amida

On the verge of transition, Qasem is invited by her brothers to a celebration and renamed Parvaneh. The arrival of the eldest brother shatters the moment — one facing a purgatory of identity, the other of denial.

25:00
3:30 PM

My Mother Bought a Camera

In 2022, filmmaker Geovana Pimentel found her mother's old 8mm camera and began a journey of self-discovery and reconciliation with her inner child.

13:34
3:45 PM

Amazon Queen 2025 Winner

A dream vacation down the Amazon becomes a hijacking. Desperate criminals storm aboard pursuing a lost fortune, and idealistic tourists — now hostages — must use every bit of ingenuity they have as food and fuel run low.

1:26:00
5:15 PM

The Building Blocks of a Bridge

One year into corporate life, a 22-year-old navigates commutes, clutter and solitude. When her mother's voice returns through a series of voicemails, small shifts begin to surface. A meditative short about estrangement and the quiet beginnings of healing.

9:44
5:30 PM

What's My Name

Set up on a blind date by a mutual friend, Rob and Rebecca are bound to get along — yet as the conversation unfolds, Rob talks the talk while Rebecca holds on to a powerful secret.

8:50
5:45 PM

Crac! 2025 Winner

A beetle abandoned by his colony lives high in an oak. Under his intrigued eye, a hiker pitches a tent at the foot of the tree while forest rangers mark the trees to be cut down with a red stroke.

14:59
6:00 PM

Rebound

A man left partially paralyzed by a serious accident turns to family, music and basketball to heal emotionally.

15:00
6:15 PM

I Am Gitxsan 2025 Winner

Phoenix Apperloo, a recent high-school graduate, reconnects with his Indigenous heritage in Hazelton, British Columbia. Inspired by residential-school survivors, he writes to the Prime Minister about the injustices faced by his people — a portrait of colonialism's impact and the enduring spirit of the Gitxsan.

1:04:00
7:30 PM

Cat-Tastrophe 2025 Winner

A cat, through its catastrophic antics, inspires a villainous inventor to devise her most evil plan yet. 3D animated slapstick.

7:45 PM

The Mosleys 2025 Winner

A touching documentary chronicling Johnny Mosley, a 100-year-old WWII veteran, and Lucile Mosley, a mother of nine, as they celebrate 77 years of marriage.

Darcine's Day 2025 Winner

A day with Darcine Thomas — divorced and jobless — as she goes from pillar to post looking for work and support, meeting obstacles at every turn, and going unseen for the help she really needs.
