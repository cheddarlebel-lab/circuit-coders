# Circuit Coders — Meta Ads Playbook ($200/mo) — researched 2026-06-29

## The honest reality
**$200/mo (~$6.50/day) is below Meta's working floor.** Meta wants ~50 optimization events/ad-set/week to "learn"; a realistic lead in our verticals costs $28–$60, so we can NEVER hit 50 leads/week. Translation: treat Meta as a **supplemental trickle + retargeting seed behind the outreach**, not a primary lead engine. Realistic output: **~3–8 qualified leads/month**. One closed setup ($1,500–$6,500) pays for 6–30 months of spend, so even 1 close/quarter is ROI-positive. Judge it at **60–90 days**, not week 1.

## The exact $200/mo setup
**Account (free, in order):** Facebook Page → Business Portfolio (business.facebook.com) → Ad Account (lock currency/timezone) → payment method → link Instagram → Pixel + one-click native Conversions API on the site.

**Campaign:**
- **Objective: Leads → on-Meta Instant Form** (keeps every dollar on-platform, no landing-page drop-off, no pixel volume needed). This is the primary.
- **Optimization:** the form submit (frequent enough to be the event). Do NOT optimize for an off-site conversion at this budget.
- **Budget:** Daily, **$6.50/day**, ONE campaign, **ONE ad set** (splitting starves everything).
- **Targeting:**
  - Geo: **multiple pin-drops over commercial/industrial corridors** (where body shops/contractors cluster) across San Diego County, North County, Temecula. Radius 5–10 mi each.
  - **Presence: "People who LIVE in this location"** (not "everyone" — that catches tourists).
  - Age 30–65, all genders.
  - **Original Audience** (geo locked). One soft interest layer max: Small business owners / Auto body / Construction / Contractor / Auto detailing. Keep audience in the tens-of-thousands — let geo filter, not interests.
- **Placements: Advantage+ (automatic)** — at this budget let Meta find the cheapest inventory (mostly Reels/Stories).
- **Creative: 2–3 ads** — the 4:5 split-screen + a 9:16 version + (strongly recommended) a phone-shot video of the bilingual AI receptionist answering a live call.
- **Instant Form:** "Higher intent" template + review step + 3 qualifiers: (1) business type, (2) "Do you own/manage the business?", (3) timeline / weekly call volume. Pre-fill name/phone/email.

## Hard rules (avoid the budget-killers)
1. ONE ad set. Never split $6.50.
2. Don't touch the ad set for 7 days after launch (edits reset learning).
3. Lock geo — never let Advantage+ expand outside the service area.
4. Don't lean on narrow interests (Meta consolidated/removed many in Jun 2025; old ones die Jan 15 2026).
5. "People who live here," not "everyone in location."
6. Pixel + CAPI both (ATT killed ~half of pixel-only signal).
7. Instant Form needs qualifiers or you get junk leads.
8. Read results at 30/60/90 days, not week 1.

## Benchmarks (US, 2025) for our space
- CPM ~$7–8 (auto/local is among the cheapest) · CPC (Traffic) ~$1 · Auto-repair CTR ~0.8% · CPL ~$28 (auto) to ~$41 (home improvement).
- $200/mo ≈ ~25k impressions, ~150–200 clicks, ~3–8 qualified leads.

## Retargeting (later)
Install pixel now; it quietly pools site visitors + video-viewers. Once ~300–500, turn on a tiny retargeting layer (180-day window) — cheapest, best-ROI lever. Build Page/IG engager + video-viewer custom audiences immediately (no site traffic needed).

## If budget ever grows
Real conversion optimization starts working around **$20–30/day**. Below that, it's a trickle by design.
