1What We Found

Verified August 4, 2026

A gambling site has taken your former name, and it is live right now

fallbrookfilmfestival.com is a live, indexed website presenting itself as the "Fallbrook International Film Festival," "celebrating independent cinema in the heart of Fallbrook, California since 2001." It lists awards — the "Frank Capra Award," the "Golden Lens Honor," the "People's Pick Trophy" — that your festival has never presented. Every outbound link on the page goes to an online casino: betgitgiris.vip and its /casino and /bonus pages, plus the affiliate tracker trk.giris.betgitportal.com.

"Fallbrook Film Festival" is the name you operated under for years, and it is still how a large share of your local audience searches for you. That search currently has a decent chance of landing a Fallbrook resident on a gambling affiliate page wearing your festival's name.

Why this matters beyond embarrassment

You are a 501(c)(3). A nonprofit's name pointing at an offshore betting operation is a sponsor-relations problem, a grant-application problem, and a reputational problem — and right now nobody is contesting it. There is a defined process (registrar abuse complaint, Google spam report, and a trademark-based UDRP claim on the strength of your prior use of the name). We file all three.

2. The entire festival lives on one page

fbiff.org is a single GoDaddy Website Builder page weighing 264 KB. Your history, the full 2025 program, the award winners, the raffle, four separate ticket links, sponsorship rates, the donation ask, the partner logos and the contact form are all stacked on that one URL.

  • There is no separate page for the program, the awards, submissions, sponsorship or the venue — so there is nothing for Google to rank for any of those searches, and nowhere specific to send an ad click.
  • 13 of the 19 images carry no alt text, which is both an accessibility failure and dead weight for search.
  • Several content blocks render with the heading "Our Programming" repeated four times in a row, where the card titles were never filled in.

3. Your phone number is missing a digit

The contact block at the bottom of fbiff.org reads (760) 638-173 — nine digits. The correct number, which appears on your own FilmFreeway listing, is (760) 638-1732. Anyone who copies the number off your website cannot call you.

4. Ticketing is four loose payment links

Passes, the guitar raffle, program-book ads and donations are each a bare square.link URL pasted into the page. There is no cart, no order confirmation that names the festival, no way to buy a pass and a raffle ticket in one transaction, and no record connecting a buyer to anything you can email later. For a one-day event, the mailing list you fail to build in 2026 is the audience you have to buy again in 2027.

5. A second domain you own is blank

fallbrookbonsallinternationalfilmfestival.org resolves, loads, and shows nothing — its page title is literally the domain name. It is splitting whatever authority your brand has instead of redirecting into it.

6. Copy that undercuts a nineteen-year-old festival

Small things, but they are the first impression a filmmaker or sponsor gets: "Clink on Link" for "Click on Link"; "PURCHASE!!!!!" and "BECOME A SPONSOR!!!!"; "The Mosley's" for "The Mosleys"; and the festival's own name written three different ways across one page.

What is working, and what we are not touching

The festival itself. The 2025 program was strong and well-paced, the six-category jury structure is right, FilmFreeway is the correct submissions platform and stays exactly where it is, Square is a perfectly good processor and stays, and the Mission Theater is a better venue story than most festivals three times your size can tell. Your tagline — Experience the Art of Cinema — is already right. The product is not the problem. The shopfront is.

4The Advertising Plan

This is an event campaign, not an always-on program. It has a hard deadline, a fixed inventory (one day, one screen) and a defined local catchment. It is paced accordingly and it ends the week after the festival.

Who we target

  • Core radius: Fallbrook, Bonsall, Rainbow, De Luz, Temecula, Murrieta, Oceanside, Vista, San Marcos, Escondido — roughly a 25-mile drive-in ring. This is a $20 day pass; nobody is driving from downtown San Diego.
  • Interests: independent film, documentary, film festivals, arthouse cinema, plus local-community and Fallbrook-specific audiences.
  • Filmmakers, targeted separately and earlier, for submissions rather than tickets — different message, different landing page.
  • Retargeting: everyone who hits the program or visit page and does not buy. Consistently the cheapest ticket you will sell.

The creative

Built at all three placement ratios so nothing gets auto-cropped. Three angles run against each other: the program (twenty-three films from around the world in one day is the actual offer, and it is a strong one), the venue (a historic single-screen house is a reason to come that a multiplex cannot copy), and the price ($20 for a full day — the closer in the final two weeks).

5The Google Ad Grant

Google gives registered 501(c)(3) nonprofits up to $10,000 per month in free Google Search advertising. You qualify: you are a 501(c)(3) in good standing with a federal tax ID (20-5921360).

The application requires a website that meets Google's policy bar — substantive content across multiple pages, a clear mission description, no broken links, secure hosting. Your current single page would very likely fail that review; the new site is built to pass it.

We prepare and file the application, set up the required conversion tracking, and build the initial campaigns. Approval typically takes two to four weeks and is not guaranteed by us or by anyone else — it is Google's decision. But it is free money for a nonprofit running an annual event, it renews every month, and it is the highest-leverage item here.

The grant carries ongoing compliance rules (a minimum click-through rate, active campaign management, an annual survey). Maintaining it is what the optional monthly retainer in section 7 is for.
