Executive Summary

Thanks for taking a few minutes when I stopped by — it was obvious right away you run a serious, high-caliber operation. Here's what I put together specifically for Ikon.

Ikon isn't a shop with a reputation problem — you're one of the best in the valley. OEM-certified for Lamborghini, Ferrari, and McLaren, I-CAR Gold Class, 4.9 stars and 300+ five-star reviews. Your work speaks for itself.

The opportunity is different for a shop at your level: when your clientele drives exotics and high-end vehicles, a single missed lead is a five-figure job. Right now there are three quiet leaks — after-hours calls, photo-upload leads that never get followed up, and local-search visibility you've earned but aren't fully claiming. This proposal plugs them.

1What We Found

What's already strong (and stays)

  • Elite reputation — 4.9★, 300+ five-star reviews, OEM exotic certifications, I-CAR Gold Class (top 10% of shops).
  • A real digital presence — clean site with a keyword-rich headline, a live chat widget already installed, and a photo/video upload estimate portal. You're ahead of most shops.

Where the high-value leaks are

  • No 24/7 phone capture. An exotic owner who just damaged a $200K car and calls after hours hits voicemail — and calls the next certified shop. At your ticket sizes, one missed call is enormous.
  • Your photo-upload portal has no automation behind it. Uploads sit in an inbox until someone opens them, and the people who upload but don't book get no follow-up. That's warm, high-intent leads going cold.
  • No LocalBusiness structured data. Despite 300+ reviews, the site is missing the schema markup Google reads for the map pack — you're leaving "exotic / aluminum repair Murrieta" ranking on the table.
  • Heavy page (~1.5 MB) — slow on mobile, where most of these searches happen.

You don't need a rebuild or a reputation fix. You need an automation layer that makes sure no high-value lead — call, upload, or search — ever slips through.
